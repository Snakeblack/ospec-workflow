# ADR-001: Independently version slice remediation

- Status: proposed
- Change: review-remediation-slices
- Date: 2026-07-22

## Context
Schema-v1 lineage identity and audit history are immutable, but its lineage-wide correction budget and atomic validation must be replaced without rewriting history.

## Decision
Keep `schema_version: 1` and its identity fields intact. Add `remediation_schema_version: 2`, a digest-bound migration record, and correction slices. A v2-aware reducer treats legacy budget/history fields as read-only audit data and requires complete v2 remediation metadata before mutable work.

## Alternatives
- Replace the whole lineage with schema v2: changes identity and complicates audit comparison.
- Move legacy histories into slices: rewrites historical records.
- Keep one version axis: couples identity evolution to remediation behavior.

## Consequences
Migration is additive, idempotent, and auditable, and rollback snapshots remain intelligible. Readers must explicitly select remediation authority; old runtimes must be barred from mutable review after migration.
